Delete your AimScor account

AimScor, by Wardon

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open AimScor.
  2. Tap your profile photo at the top right to open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email (no app needed)

Email us from the Google account you signed in with. Write "Delete my AimScor account" as the subject. We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Email aimscor15@gmail.com

What gets deleted

  • Your account and account ID
  • Your Google email address, name and profile photo link
  • Your daily count of online double-checks

What is kept

  • Nothing about your account is kept on our server, with one exception: if an account was blocked for abuse, its email address stays on our block list.
  • Short technical server logs are removed automatically within a few days.
  • Tournaments saved on your phone are not on our server. They stay on your phone until you delete them in the app or uninstall AimScor.

Read the full privacy policy.
