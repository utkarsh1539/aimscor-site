AimScor

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

This policy explains what data the AimScor Android app (package com.aimscor.app) collects, why, and how you can delete it. AimScor is made by Wardon ("we", "us"). Questions: aimscor15@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your tournaments, teams, points and posters are stored only on your phone.
  • Screenshots are read on your phone. They leave the phone only if you tap "Double-check online", and they are not stored.
  • If you sign in with Google, we keep your email address, name and an account ID so the app works and you can delete your account.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your data.

Data we collect

DataWhenWhy
Google account email, name and profile photo linkWhen you choose "Sign in with Google"To create and identify your account, show who is signed in, and let you delete the account
Account ID (a random identifier)When you sign in with Google or continue as a guestTo run your account and keep the app's AI features working
Screenshots you chooseOnly when you tap "Double-check online" (signed-in accounts)Sent to our server and read by Google's Gemini service to extract ranks, team names and kills. They are processed in memory and not stored by us.
Daily count of online double-checksEach time you use "Double-check online"To apply a fair daily limit and protect the service from abuse
Google Play Integrity verdictWhen the app unlocks AI scanTo check that the app is the genuine version from Google Play on a real device (protects against modified copies). The verdict is checked and not stored.

Data that stays on your phone

Tournaments, team lists, match results, player kills, poster designs and settings are saved on your device only. We do not receive them. When you share a poster, a table or an .aimscor file, it goes only to the app or person you choose.

Permissions

AimScor uses internet access (sign-in, unlocking AI scan, online double-check). You pick screenshots with the Android photo picker, so the app does not get access to your whole gallery. Saving a poster adds that one image to your gallery.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: sign-in and our server (stores the account data listed above).
  • Google: Sign in with Google, the Gemini API (reading screenshots for "Double-check online"), Google Play Integrity and Google Play.

We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Screenshots sent for "Double-check online" are not kept after they are read.
  • Our server keeps short technical logs for a few days to fix errors and stop abuse.
  • If an account was blocked for abuse, we keep its email address on a block list after deletion, so the block still applies.

Delete your account and data

In the app: tap your profile photo (top right) → Delete account. Or follow the steps on Delete your AimScor account. Uninstalling the app removes the tournaments saved on your phone.

Security

All data between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The key that unlocks AI scan is kept in Android's secure storage on your phone.

Children

AimScor is meant for tournament organisers aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
